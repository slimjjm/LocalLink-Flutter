Privacy Policy

LocalLink helps people discover local activities, services, availability and community help. We collect only the information needed to run those features safely.

Information we use

This may include account details, profile details you choose to add, public post content, booking details, messages, notification preferences, approximate local area, device tokens for notifications, and technical logs needed to keep the service reliable.

Location

LocalLink uses location to show nearby content and alerts. Public posts should use approximate location labels. We do not need to publish precise home addresses or exact coordinates for Community Help alerts.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. LocalLink does not store full card numbers.

Your choices

You can manage notification preferences in the app, edit profile information, and request account deletion.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@locallinkapp.co.uk.
