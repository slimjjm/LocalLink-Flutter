Delete Account

You can request deletion of your LocalLink account and associated personal data.

Fastest option

Open LocalLink, go to settings, and choose delete account. This helps us confirm the request belongs to you.

Email request

If you cannot access the app, email support@locallinkapp.co.uk from the email address linked to your LocalLink account and ask for account deletion.

Some records may be retained where required for legal, safety, payment, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution reasons.
